### Research

#### Research Findings: ste-cnl-specification-engine

##### Scope & Problem Context
The issue `feat--ste-cnl-specification-engine` introduces Controlled Natural Language (STE-inspired) requirement format for cross-runtime handoff.
Writing convention, not a gate:
1. `REQ-N` pattern: `[Subject] + MUST | MUST NOT + [one verifiable action] + [object] + [condition]`.
2. Issue template shows the pattern in `## Requirements` instead of placeholder text.
3. Architect role writes requirements in this format; `along brief` passes them unchanged to implementer and reviewer.
4. Guide `docs/topic--requirement-format.md` (type: guide): pattern, banned words, 3 good and 3 bad examples.
5. Out of scope / non-goals: no runtime gate for requirement wording.
